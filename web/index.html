Play without browser bars

This browser cannot enter fullscreen here. On iPhone or iPad, open the game from your Home Screen to remove Safari’s address and navigation bars.

  1. In Safari, open Share (it may be inside the More menu).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Launch Domeblast 3D from its new Home Screen icon, then turn your phone sideways.

On other phones, look for Install app or Add to Home Screen in the browser menu. Home Screen play still needs an internet connection. Your device may keep its system status or home indicator visible.

Flight controls

On phones, use both on-screen sticks together: left to move, right to aim and fire. AUTO toggles continuous fire, the curved arrows rotate the camera, and Ⅱ pauses. Touch controls can be shown or hidden below.

On Quest, choose Enter VR to use the Touch controller sticks in a seated tabletop view. Outside VR, use a regular Bluetooth gamepad or keyboard; Touch sticks are not exposed to the flat page. Left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger fires. Squeeze the left or right side grip trigger to rotate the arena while playing; squeeze farther to turn faster. The right index trigger still fires. B pauses, X toggles autofire, Y recenters the table and resets its rotation. Use the left stick and A to navigate VR menus.
